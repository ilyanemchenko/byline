My mornings used to start with my phone. Email, news, notifications, and by the time I got to my first real task, I had already spent my best energy reacting to other people's priorities.

So I changed the order. Now the first hour belongs to me.

What it looks like:

- I get up at the same time most days, even on weekends. Consistency matters more than the exact hour.
- Water and a few minutes of quiet before anything else. No screen for the first 20 minutes.
- A short walk, or at least a few minutes outside. It clears my head faster than coffee does.
- I write down the one or two things that would make the day feel like a win. Not a long list, just the essentials.
- Then I open my laptop and start with the hardest of those tasks, while my focus is still fresh.

None of this is revolutionary. The difference is that I decide the start of the day instead of letting the inbox decide it for me.

I still miss some mornings. When I do, I don't try to fix the whole routine. I just come back to it the next day.

What does your first hour look like? I am always curious how other people protect their focus.
